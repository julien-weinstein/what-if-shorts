# Uploads

Copy-paste text for YouTube. Upload each MP4 from `videos/` in **YouTube Studio → Create → Upload videos**. A vertical video under 3 minutes automatically becomes a Short.

## Channel setup (once)

- **Profile picture:** `art/avatar.png`
- **Banner:** `art/banner.png`
- **Description:**

  > What if the Moon vanished? What if Earth stopped spinning? What if the Sun went out?
  > Earth-ending scenarios, animated in under a minute, with real science behind every frame.
  > New what-if every day. 🌍💥

- **Keywords:** what if, hypothetical, science, space, earth, apocalypse, end of the world, planets, animation, shorts

On each upload, answer **"No, it's not made for kids"**. That keeps comments on, and comments drive the Shorts feed. The "altered or synthetic content" question is about realistic footage, so for cartoon animation answer **No**.

**Description:**

```
It would be the most beautiful thing you've ever seen... for about a day.

#whatif #moon #space #physics #shorts
```

**Pinned comment:** The math: tidal force grows with the CUBE of distance. 10x closer = 1,000x the tides. At the breaking point it's nearly 10,000x. 🌊

## 3. What if a planet the size of Mars hit Earth?

**File:** `videos/planet-impact.mp4`

**Title:** What If a Planet the Size of Mars Hit Earth? ☄️🌍 #shorts

**Description:**

```
Weeks out, telescopes spot it. On the last night it fills the sky. In the final hour its gravity drags the ocean upward.

At 40,000 km/h it hits with 100 MILLION times the energy of the asteroid that killed the dinosaurs.
A shockwave laps the planet. Molten debris rains down everywhere. The oceans boil like soup (that takes less than 0.01% of the energy).
Landmarks melt like candle wax. Even deep bunkers fail: the crust melts hundreds of kilometers down.
Within a day (est.): all 8.2 billion of us.

Earth becomes a ball of magma, the debris forms a ring, then clumps into a new moon. Millions of years later, the rain brings the oceans back.

Plot twist: this already happened. 4.5 billion years ago, a planet called Theia hit the young Earth. Its wreckage became our Moon.

#whatif #space #physics #moon #shorts
```

**Pinned comment:** The math: half × Mars's mass × (11.2 km/s)² ≈ 4×10³¹ joules. The dinosaur asteroid was about 4×10²³. 🌋

## Posting rhythm

- Post one a day at a consistent time. Late afternoon in your main audience's time zone is a good default.
- Reply to early comments in the first hour. It noticeably helps a Short get pushed further.
- Post the same files to TikTok and Instagram Reels. There's no voice and the audio is original, so they can be reposted as they are.
